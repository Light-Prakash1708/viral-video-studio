Can you swap bodies like Your Name? In 2008, scientists made people feel it. Your brain can lose track of whose body is yours. Here's how.
Two people face each other. One wears goggles showing a live camera feed, from the other person's head.
They shake hands, squeezing at the same moment.
Soon, volunteers said the other person's arm felt like their own. They were watching themselves... shake their own hand.
Then came a knife. Held to the borrowed arm, stress jumped. Held to their real arm? Less. Small study. Wild result.
Why? Your brain doesn't know where your body is. It guesses, from what you see and feel at the same moment. Match them, and the guess moves.
It even works on a rubber hand. But not on a chair. Your brain only moves into something shaped like a body.
And the part where Taki and Mitsuha forget each other's names? That's real too. Most dreams fade within minutes of waking.
So... whose body are you in right now?
