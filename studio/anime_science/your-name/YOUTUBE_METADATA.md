# Your Name body swap: upload text
**Title:** Scientists Made the Your Name Body Swap Real (Sort Of)
Alternatives: "Your Name's Body Swap Is Real Science" · "Can You Swap Bodies Like Your Name?"

**Description:**
Taki and Mitsuha swapped bodies. In 2008, scientists made people feel it too. 🌠

With goggles fed by a camera on someone else's head and a synchronised handshake, volunteers started to feel another person's arm was their own. Their stress response jumped when a knife came near the borrowed arm, and was about 30% lower for their real arm (small study).

So, whose body are you in right now? 👇
