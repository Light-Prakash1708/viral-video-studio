## 2. Script (v2 — punch-up)
**HOOK 0–6 s**
ANKUSH: Smart girls fall for dumb boys. It's science.
PRIYA: It is not— *(beat)* …okay. It's a *little* bit science.
`TITLE SLAM: WHY SMART GIRLS FALL FOR "DUMB" BOYS`

**SITUATION 6–15 s**
PRIYA: Exhibit A. Wrote "Wensday" on my birthday card.
ANKUSH: That's how it's pronounced.
PRIYA: Spellcheck filed a restraining order.

**SCORECARD 15–30 s**
PRIYA: So I made a scorecard. Academics: two.
ANKUSH: Out of ten?
PRIYA: Out of a hundred. Punctuality: three. Common sense: under investigation.
ANKUSH: Put "handsome."
PRIYA: This is *science.*
ANKUSH: Science has eyes.

**FIRST CRACK 30–37 s**
`SPLIT SCREEN — cat falls off a shelf / Priya snort-laughs`
ANKUSH: You laughed. Write it down.
PRIYA: *(through teeth)* Humour… pending.

**SCIENCE 37–55 s**
PRIYA: Here's the annoying part. Being funny is linked to intelligence.
ANKUSH: So I'm a genius.
PRIYA: Linked. Not guaranteed. And in surveys, women say they want someone who makes them laugh—
ANKUSH: Me.
PRIYA: —and men want someone who laughs at *their* jokes.
ANKUSH: Perfect system. I talk, you laugh.
PRIYA: Said. Not did.

**TWIST 55–68 s**
PRIYA: But in one study, funny people got picked as partners… and rated *less* intelligent.
ANKUSH: I've been profiled.
`SCORECARD GLITCH: ERROR — WRONG TEST`
ANKUSH: *(grabs her notebook)* Wait. "I'd tell a chemistry joke, but I wouldn't get a reaction"?
PRIYA: Delete that.
ANKUSH: There are *forty pages* of these.
PRIYA: I'm selectively ridiculous.

**PAYOFF 68–80 s**
PRIYA: Smart girls don't fall for dumb boys. They fall for the one who laughs at their worst jokes.
ANKUSH: …So I win.
PRIYA: You still can't spell Wednesday.
ANKUSH: Put "handsome."
`END CARD: TOON THEORY — PEOPLE ARE WEIRD. LET'S EXPLAIN WHY.`
`COMMENT PROMPT: Rate Ankush out of 10. Wrong answers only.`

### What changed vs. the owner draft, and why
- **Ragebait hook:** Ankush states the stereotype as fact and Priya *half-concedes*. The argument starts in second 1 and the comments fight about it. The title's quotes around "dumb" and the payoff dismantle it, so the stereotype is never endorsed.
- **Three running gags** that pay off at the end: "Wensday", "Put 'handsome'", the scorecard.
- **Narrator removed** (duo format). The takeaway moves to Priya.
- **Science lines turned into banter.** Every finding gets an Ankush interruption plus a 3-word caveat, so it's accurate without a lecture.
- **The twist is a real finding (R3)**, not just a plot reveal. Then the notes reveal flips it onto Priya (she's caught out too).
- **Loop ending:** the last line repeats the bit from 0:20, so rewatches feel seamless.
