**Title:** Why Smart Girls Fall for "Dumb" Boys
**Description:**
Top of the class. Spells Wednesday "Wensday". So why does she like him? Priya runs the numbers on Ankush… and the scorecard breaks.

Research mentioned (summaries simplified; see the limits):
• Greengross, G. & Miller, G. (2011). Humor ability reveals intelligence, predicts mating success, and is higher in males. Intelligence, 39(4), 188–192. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intell.2011.03.006 — about 400 students; higher verbal and general intelligence went along with funnier cartoon captions. A correlation, not proof that funny = smart.
• Bressler, E. R. & Balshine, S. (2006). The influence of humor on desirability. Evolution and Human Behavior, 27(1), 29–39. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.evolhumbehav.2005.06.002 — in one experiment, women picked humorous men as partners more often. One study, on average; not a universal rule.
Fictional comedy, not relationship advice.
