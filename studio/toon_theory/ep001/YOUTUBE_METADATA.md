## Description
Eleven drafts to say "hey". Top of her class. So why can't Priya stop waiting?

Two friends watch a stranger's text spiral and work out what's really keeping her hooked: not the guy, but the maybe, the picture of who he could be, and the hope of finally being chosen.

THE RELATIONSHIP TRAP · Episode 1 of 3. Next: why you can feel single while you're in a relationship.

What the story draws on (details and limits):
• Uncertainty about being liked can increase attraction: Whitchurch, Wilson & Gilbert (2011), Psychological Science, https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797610393745. One small study of college women, and a replication attempt did not find the effect, so treat it as a clue, not a rule.
• Investment makes relationships harder to leave: Le & Agnew (2003) meta-analysis, https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6811.00035; Rusbult (1980), https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1031(80)90007-4.
• Seeing the best in a partner is linked to happier couples: Murray, Holmes & Griffin (1996), https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.70.1.79 and https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.71.6.1155. Correlational findings.
"She wants to be picked by him" is a storytelling interpretation, not a research finding. This is a fictional story, not therapy or relationship advice.
