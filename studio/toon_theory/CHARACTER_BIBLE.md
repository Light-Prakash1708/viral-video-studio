# TOON THEORY: PERMANENT CHARACTER BIBLE

Status: **pre-production, awaiting creative review.** Source of truth for visuals: `characters/generate_characters.py` (one parametric definition per character; regenerate sheets with `python3 generate_characters.py && node render.cjs`). Never hand-edit output SVG/PNG.

## Global rules
- **Fictional characters.** Krish and Ankush are fictional, inspired only by names. No trait, appearance or backstory is assumed from a name.
- **No cultural identity from names.** Skin tone, ethnicity and background are stylised and deliberately unspecified.
- **English only** (natural, contemporary, international). Every character sounds different; none sounds like the narrator.
- **Consistency lock.** Facial structure, hairstyle, clothing identity, palette and proportions never change between episodes. A costume change needs an explicit bible revision.
- **Style.** Slightly stylised 2D figures, about 6 heads tall (head scaled 1.22 for expressiveness), clean outline-free shapes, in layered cinematic environments with **warm orange rim light from the left and cool blue rim light from the right**, deep shadows and atmospheric bokeh.
- **Honest limit.** Our toolchain makes flat 2D characters in layered, lit scenes with parallax and camera moves. It does not make rigged 3D models.

## KRISH (permanent recurring)
- **Age range:** 21–23. **Role:** observant analyst of other people's behaviour who can't see his own.
- **Personality:** observant, intelligent, analytical, overthinks, kind under the dryness.
- **Strengths:** notices small emotional details, patient, honest when pressed.
- **Weaknesses:** overthinks, hesitates, hides behind analysis, avoids acting on his own feelings.
- **Humour:** dry and understated, often self-undercutting.
- **Speech patterns:** short, careful sentences; "I think", "don't quote me", trailing "...". Never a lecture.
- **Emotional tendencies:** goes quiet when something matters; deflects with observation.
- **Clothing and accessories:** sage-green overshirt (#7d9a7b) over charcoal tee (#3b3f48), black jeans, grey sneakers, cool-blue earbud cable around his neck.
- **Face:** soft round-oval face, heavy-lidded calm eyes, medium straight brows, short curly dark hair (#17110e), no facial hair.
- **Body language:** lanky, rounded shoulders, hands in sleeves, still while thinking, stirs things when he overthinks.
- **Voice:** dry, quiet, exact, mid-low. Casting recommendation below.
- **Relationships:** Ankush is his closest friend and flatmate; each exposes the other's contradictions. Neither is hero or villain.
- **Development:** learns that analysing a feeling isn't the same as acting on it (four drafts in Ep 001).

## ANKUSH (permanent recurring)
- **Age range:** 21–23. **Role:** confident, spontaneous friend who challenges Krish and is sometimes right.
- **Personality:** confident, sociable, spontaneous, impulsive, warm.
- **Strengths:** reads a room fast, loyal, funny, sincere when it counts.
- **Weaknesses:** confident conclusions that are sometimes wrong, impulsive, avoids stillness.
- **Humour:** quick comebacks, playful sarcasm, teasing.
- **Speech patterns:** fast, bright, interrupts, punchy short lines; gets gentle when serious.
- **Emotional tendencies:** jokes first, feels second; softens when a friend is hurt.
- **Clothing and accessories:** brick-red varsity jacket (#b23a2e) with cream sleeve stripe, white tee, washed jeans, white high-tops.
- **Face:** broad squarer jaw, thick expressive brows, light stubble, sharp-fade dark hair (#120d0b) with textured top.
- **Body language:** big gestures, leans back with arms spread, takes up space, steals Krish's fries.
- **Voice:** fast, bright, playful.
- **Relationships:** Krish (flatmate and sounding board).
- **Development:** learns to sit with an unresolved feeling without a joke.

## PRIYA (Episode 001 lead)
- **Age range:** 22. **Role:** brilliant student stuck waiting on an inconsistent relationship.
- **Personality:** intelligent, ambitious, precise, over-explains when nervous, quietly funny.
- **Strengths:** disciplined, perceptive, loyal.
- **Weaknesses:** treats uncertainty as a puzzle to solve; wants to be chosen.
- **Humour:** self-deprecating, precise, dry when relaxed.
- **Speech patterns:** full sentences, speeds up when anxious, trails off when unsure.
- **Emotional tendencies:** hope, then self-doubt; goes very still when she finally listens.
- **Clothing:** cream oversized cardigan (#efe2c8), mustard top (#e0a526), indigo jeans, white sneakers, teal tote (#2a8f93) with notebooks, gold studs, teal claw clip.
- **Face:** oval face, wide attentive eyes, soft arched brows, long dark hair (#2a1a17) with a centre part.
- **Body language:** hunched, hugs notebook, fidgets thumbs, leans toward her phone.
- **Voice:** clear, mid-range, precise.
- **Relationships:** Meera (best friend), Rohan (the pull), Aman (friend who asks).
- **Development:** recognises she was writing to someone who doesn't exist.

## MEERA (Episode 001 friend)
- **Age range:** 22. **Role:** the friend who says it straight.
- **Personality:** blunt, funny, perceptive, protective.
- **Strengths:** honesty, loyalty, timing.
- **Weaknesses:** can say "I told you so" too early.
- **Humour:** deadpan.
- **Speech patterns:** short, flat, one-word replies ("Don't.", "Finally.").
- **Emotional tendencies:** protective sarcasm that drops when Priya is truly hurt.
- **Clothing:** denim jacket (#4f79a8), burgundy tee (#8a2a3d), charcoal cargos, black boots, gold hoops.
- **Face:** round face, strong straight brows (one eyebrow does the work), chin-length black bob with side-swept bangs (#16121a).
- **Body language:** arms crossed or one hand on hip, weight on one leg, leans back.
- **Voice:** low, flat, quick.
- **Relationships:** Priya (best friend).

## ROHAN (Episode 001)
- **Age range:** 23–24. **Role:** charming and inconsistent; present or absent, never a villain.
- **Personality:** warm, socially easy, genuinely attentive in the moment, avoids follow-through.
- **Strengths:** makes people feel seen; funny; remembers small things.
- **Weaknesses:** vague, deflects, avoids commitment to a plan.
- **Humour:** easy, self-mocking.
- **Speech patterns:** relaxed, loose, laughs mid-sentence ("I know, I know").
- **Emotional tendencies:** warm when present, gone the next day.
- **Clothing:** camel suede jacket (#c4955f), black tee, dark jeans, off-white sneakers, thin silver chain.
- **Face:** easy half-lidded eyes, tousled dark hair (#1c130f), light stubble.
- **Body language:** open, leans in, busy hands, eyes everywhere.
- **Voice:** warm, easy, a little vague.

## AMAN (supporting)
- **Age range:** 22–24. **Role:** a friend who asks the follow-up question; **not** a romantic solution.
- **Personality:** thoughtful, specific, dependable.
- **Clothing:** navy knit cardigan (#2b3a5c), sky shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes, round glasses, canvas tote with a yellow bike-light clip.
- **Body language:** upright and attentive, listens with his whole face.
- **Voice:** text-only in Episode 001.

## Expression library (all characters)
Neutral, smile, laugh, deadpan, worried, stunned, smirk, hurt, generated from the same face rig so every character can hit every expression.

## Lighting and colour system
- **Warm orange** (#ff8c3c family) = presence, imagined potential, the phone's glow.
- **Cool blue** (#4682ff family) = absence, distance, silence.
- Backgrounds: deep plum and navy, soft bokeh, vignette. Characters carry a left-orange and right-blue rim.
