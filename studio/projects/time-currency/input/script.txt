A billionaire has two hundred years. A poor man has twenty. Would that be a fair economy? Imagine a world where money is gone, and time is the only currency. Every person is born with a clock on their wrist. Twenty five years. That's it. Coffee costs three minutes. Rent costs a month of your life. A bus ride, an hour. The rich never run out. They buy centuries, and live forever in glass towers above the city. The poor wake up every morning with one day left. They work to earn tomorrow, and spend it just to stay alive. A mother gives her son her last hour, so he can make it home. Nobody can be robbed of money anymore. But everyone can be robbed of life. And here's the uncomfortable part. Maybe that world isn't so different from ours. We already trade our hours for money. Forty hours a week, for decades. So what is your time really worth? If you had to pay for everything in time, what would you stop buying? Tell me in the comments.
