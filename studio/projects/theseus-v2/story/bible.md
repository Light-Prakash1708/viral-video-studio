# The Ship of Theseus — bible
Logline: a ship rebuilt plank by plank asks whether you are still you. Tone: wonder → mystery → reflection.
Cast: THESEUS (red tunic, curly hair), WRIGHT (blue tee, gold headband), PLUTARCH (white robe), PHIL_A (green, "same"), PHIL_B (pink, bun, "new"), HOBBES (dark coat), YOU (purple hoodie; kid version in the mirror).
Sets: harbor (dusk, sun over the sea, Acropolis silhouette), shipyard (golden / dusk timber shed), colonnade (golden god rays), study (night window + candle), bedroom (night, mirror), blueprint.
Motif: planks glow and hop as they swap from dark old wood to bright new wood; the same swap appears in cells.
Music: mystery → wonder → tension → hopeful.
