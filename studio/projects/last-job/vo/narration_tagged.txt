[softly] In the year 2061... there was exactly one human being on Earth who still had a job.

His name was Walter Hayes. He was seventy-eight years old. [warmly] And he sold coffee.

Everything else had been handed to the machines. Robots drove the trains. Robots taught the kids. Robots did the surgery, wrote the songs, and cooked every meal. Eight billion people, and not one of them had to work. The government paid everyone. Nobody was hungry. [pause] And nobody, it seemed, was needed.

Except for one small coffee cart, at the end of Track Four, in a train station the trains no longer stopped at.

The sign said, "Walt's Coffee. Since 1999."

Across the street, a machine made perfect coffee in three seconds. For free. [curious] And still, every morning at six, twelve people stood in line for Walt.

The Department of Efficiency noticed. [serious] So they sent Unit M-Seven, to close the last human job on Earth.

[pause]

So M-Seven stayed. And it watched.

On Monday, Mr. Bennett came. He had lost his wife the winter before. Walt didn't ask how he was doing. He just set down two cinnamon cookies. [softly] Her favorite. Without a word.

On Tuesday, a young guy named Arjun came. He hadn't left his apartment in months. Walt tossed him a dish towel. "Mugs won't wash themselves, kid." Arjun stayed until noon. [warmly] He smiled for the first time in a year.

On Wednesday, it was Maya. Ten years old. Big math test.

[pause]

Every night, M-Seven wrote its report. Monday said, "Inefficiency detected." Tuesday said, "Inefficiency detected." [curious] By Friday... it said something new. "Pattern unknown."

On Sunday, the seventh day, the report was due.

[pause] But the cart was closed.

[sad] Walt was in the hospital. His heart was tired. It had been working for seventy-eight years, after all.

M-Seven stood by the bed. It had a question.

Walt smiled.

[pause] At six the next morning, the cart on Track Four opened.

[warmly] Mr. Bennett got two cinnamon cookies. Arjun got a dish towel. And Maya's hot chocolate had extra marshmallows... and a note, in shaky robot handwriting. "Test day. Walt says, you've got this."

That evening, M-Seven sent its report to the Department. It was one line long.

[pause]

And on the cart, it hung a new sign.

[softly] "Help wanted. Humans only."
