Three prisoners. Five hats. Three white, two black. Guess your hat right, you walk free. Guess wrong... you don't.

The guard lines them up, one behind the other, facing a wall. Each gets a hat he can't see.

Prisoner three, at the back, sees both hats in front of him. He stays silent.

Prisoner two sees one hat. He stays silent too.

Prisoner one sees nothing. Just the wall.

And yet... he's the one who answers. And he's right.

What color is his hat? Beat the clock.

Time's up. It's white.

If prisoner three had seen two black hats, he'd know his was white. He stayed silent. So at least one front hat is white.

Prisoner two knows that too. If prisoner one's hat were black, his own would have to be white. But he stayed silent.

So prisoner one's hat... is white.

The silence was the clue. Comment if you beat the clock.
