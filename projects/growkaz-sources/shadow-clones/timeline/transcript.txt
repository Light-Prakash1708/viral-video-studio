Naruto's shadow clones sound like a cheat code, but a thousand clones would break your brain. Here's the catch. When a clone vanishes, everything it learned rushes back into you. One clone training for an hour? Easy! A thousand clones? A thousand hours of memories hitting you at once. Over 40 days of experience in a single second. Your brain isn't built for that. Working memory holds about four things at a time. Four, not four hundred. Your senses collect around a billion bits every second. Your conscious mind handles about 10. So a thousand clones isn't a thousand times stronger. It's a flood through a straw. The real limit isn't chakra. It's attention. So how many clones could your brain handle?
