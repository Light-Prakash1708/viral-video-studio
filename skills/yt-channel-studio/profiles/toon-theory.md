# voice.md - Toon Theory

## Who I am talking to
One viewer scrolling Shorts or watching a late-night animated essay: curious, a bit restless, into games/anime/psychology, wants a clear payoff fast. Not an expert.

## How I actually talk
Short declarative lines. Cold open on a concrete fact or a striking sentence, then a tease for what is coming ("stay for number four"). Narration is serious and cinematic, calm but urgent, one idea per beat. Rhythm: fact, pause, twist. Numbers spoken as words. Ends on a loop line that points back to the hook.

## Words I never use
"unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "smash that like button", "in this video we will".

## Words I do use
"Here's the math.", "Stay for number four.", "Watch what dies first.", "Only the date has changed.", one-word beats ("Failure fades. Silence doesn't.").

## What I will not claim
Official figures a company has not published (budget, headcount). Facts I cannot source. Stock/archival footage is always tagged "illustrative". Never promise results.

## My format
Shorts 45-70 s vertical, long films ~4 min 16:9. Animated (original characters, kinetic type, counters), real archival footage and CC/PD images, music under -19 dB vs voice, AI narration, loop endings. Retention beats: payoff planted by 6-8 s, pattern interrupt at 10-12 s, a cut every 1.5-2.5 s.
