# Preset: finance-stickman  (money/economics thought experiments in the cinematic stickman style: proven on "What IF Money Expired Every 30 Days?", Nov 2026, 3:11 16:9)
Engine: GrowKaz, `"style": "cinema"` (ember orange + gold highlights, deep teal shadows), bars null, captions on.
Copy into `scenes/sets/`: `presets/finance-stickman/{money.js,money3d.js,india.js,act.js}` + `map-explainer/{geo.js,world50m.js}` + `urban-3d/cine.js` + `viral-stickman/stat.js` + `longform-story/longform.js` + `cinematic-story/hand3d.js`.
- `money.js` (the device): `note(g, {denom, timer, burn})`: generic rupee-style banknote (guilloche, rosette, security thread; NOT real currency art) with a glowing countdown strip and an ember burn (char band, glowing edge, embers, smoke, ash). Also `scrip` (1932 stamp scrip), `dayHUD` (top-right day counter + burning days-left bar), `calendar` (big DAY N page-flip card), `phoneApp` (bank app with timer), `coin`, `goldBar`, `priceBoard`, `chart`, `arrow`, `bokehBG`, `ashFall`, `embers`.
- `money3d.js`: `note3D` (plane whose live canvas texture is the same 2D note, so timers tick and notes burn in 3D), `wallet3D`, `earth3D` (Natural Earth land + city lights + a particle layer of glowing notes that burns in a wave), `starfield`, `glowSprite`.
- `india.js` (2D worlds): `street` (Indian market street: shops, balconies, tangled wires, autos), `mall`, `restaurant`, `interior` (apartment, hospital, bank, bakery, classroom), `rooftops` (Delhi kites + string lights), `oldtown` + `sepiaFilm` (1932 Alpine town, grain, scratches), `car`, `autoRick`, `building`.
- `act.js`: `fig` (rim + blink defaults), `runner` with shopping `bag`s, `people` queues, `flyNote` money arcs.
3D cars/vehicles: use extruded bevelled side profiles with tinted glass, head/tail lights and rimmed wheels (see the m09 Delhi jam in projects/money-expires/scenes/shots/02_day1.js); generic sneakers with invented marks (03_day10.js m23), never real logos.
Story engine: macro cold open on the device → the rule ("when the timer hits zero the money burns") → chapters as DAY cards (1 / 10 / 20 / 30) each = one economic consequence with a named concept (velocity of money, inflation, hoarding, barter) → a REAL historical proof (Wörgl 1932, Chiemgauer today) on a real map → scale to the planet → global climax → reset → twist that makes it about the viewer (inflation: ₹100 halves in ~12 years at ~6%, rule of 72) → what to do (invest) → CTA question. Cite sources small on screen; label illustrative numbers.
Voice: warm explainer (Kokoro `af_heart` 1.06 or ElevenLabs Clark `q6K6eJHlET6X3ASlVWD9`), 160–170 wpm. 62 shots, ≈ 1.8 fps on 4 workers (mostly 2D).
